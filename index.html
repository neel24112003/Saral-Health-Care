About the hospital

Care built around the patient, not the paperwork

Saral Health Care is a multi-speciality hospital and diagnostic centre serving Surat, built on the idea that good healthcare should feel simple — clear communication, fast diagnostics, and specialists who actually have time for you.

From routine consultations to emergency care, every department shares one patient record, one lab network and one care team — so nothing gets lost between departments.
